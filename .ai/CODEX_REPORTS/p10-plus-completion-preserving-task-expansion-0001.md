# P10+ Completion-Preserving Task Expansion 0001

## Canonical Rule

Human-authorized long-run programs should complete one coherent objective instead of fragmenting necessary follow-up steps into many micro-tasks.

When Codex discovers cleanup, validation, alignment, report, or repair steps that are necessary for the original objective and remain inside the authorized scope, those steps are folded into the current program's internal stage graph, checkpoints, issue log, repair cohort, and final review package.

## Escalation Boundary

Codex must still stop and escalate for:

- new Human authority;
- objective change;
- forbidden paths;
- destructive Git;
- source or master push;
- secrets, privacy, or security risk;
- Coordinator or runtime activation;
- whole-task blockers.
